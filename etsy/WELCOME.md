# The Slow Atlas — Welcome / Message to Buyers

Paste this into **Shop Manager → Settings → Info & Appearance → Message to Buyers**
(it's sent automatically on every order). English version is primary (your shop and
files are in English); a French version follows if you'd like to greet French buyers.

---

## English (recommended)

> **Bonjour, and welcome to The Slow Atlas!** 🌍
>
> Thank you so much for your order — it genuinely means the world to a small shop
> like mine.
>
> **Your planner is ready now:** head to *Purchases → Download Files* in your Etsy
> account. You'll find both a **US Letter** and an **A4** PDF — use whichever suits
> you. Open it in any PDF reader (or a tablet app like GoodNotes) to type straight
> in, or print it as often as you like. It's yours to reuse for every trip to come.
>
> A few quick things:
> • Any trouble opening or printing it? Just message me — I'm always happy to help.
> • Loving it? A quick review would truly make my day and helps other travellers
>   find the shop. ✨
> • These files are for personal use only — please don't resell or share them.
>
> Now the fun part: go plan something wonderful. Bon voyage! 🧳
>
> — Marin · The Slow Atlas · Rennes, France
